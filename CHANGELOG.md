# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

- First release: an offline, portable monkeytype recreation (single-file `MonkeyType.exe`, WebView2 shell, data in
  `MonkeyType-Data\`).
- Time, words, quote, zen and custom modes, with punctuation and numbers. English 200/1k/5k/10k word sets and 6,488
  quotes.
- monkeytype scoring (wpm, raw, accuracy, consistency), result chart, PBs, words history and practise of missed
  words.
- 186 themes, a custom theme editor, settings page, command line, and a local stats and history page with
  JSON/CSV export.
